A SMALL SURPRISE

A little note
for Juliet.

This is a special page made with care. The message can be personalized before the gift is shared.

FROM YOUR FRIEND

Dear Juliet,

I wanted to make something that was yours—a little place for your words to live beyond the pages, a place where someone might stumble upon a poem and feel understood. Your writing deserves room to grow, to be read, and to stay.

May this be the beginning of many words, many stories, and many people finding a little piece of themselves in what you write.

With care, and in celebration of your words.
